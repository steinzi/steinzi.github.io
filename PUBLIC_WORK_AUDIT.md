## Purpose and editorial approach

This is a career-wide audit, not a scrape of every social post. It records durable work that a reasonable visitor can verify: career milestones, talks, workshops, podcast appearances and episodes, articles, open-source releases, education, and community initiatives.

The website uses that evidence to tell one coherent story:

1. Steinn started with electronics, physical security, IT services, and production networks.
2. He built deep operational credibility, including CCIE #60715, while teaching and studying computer science at night.
3. He moved from operating systems to designing automation products and replacing legacy tooling.
4. He now applies that production mindset to AI automation across infrastructure and business processes, with security, human approval, and adoption designed in.

This is more useful than presenting a very long list of technologies and asking the visitor to assemble a personality from it.
